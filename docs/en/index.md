---
title: Lock screen
description: Configure the Hyprlock-based ARGVUS lock screen.
---

`argvus-lock` supplies the Hyprlock configuration and theme helper. Its packaged files are under `/usr/share/argvus/lock/config/` and `/usr/share/argvus/lock/sh/`.

`argvus-power` and the idle services invoke locking and DPMS actions. Theme changes regenerate the lock-screen appearance.
